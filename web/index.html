How to play

The ball bounces up and down on its own. You steer it left and right. Clear every diamond to finish a level, but diamonds only break once all the plain bricks are gone, and a plain brick only breaks when your brush (shown on the right of the screen) matches it. Hit a paintbrush brick, the one with a dark right edge, to change your brush. Keys open locks, double arrows swap left and right, and crosses kill you.

Enter / SpaceStart, then pause and resume during a game
A B CMenu: play, change speed, show keys
← →Steer the ball
BackspaceRestart the level (costs a life)
ShiftGive up the current game
M / -Sound on/off
EscExit (the calculator's ON key)

Credits

Diamonds 38 by Douglas R. Cannon (1993, ported to the HP 38G in 1996-97) and Detlef Mueller (1998), based on Oliver Dreer's Diamonds for the Macintosh. Released as freeware with source; downloaded from hpcalc.org. This browser version is a line-by-line port of the original Saturn assembly: the levels, graphics, ball physics, timing and beeps all come straight from it.
